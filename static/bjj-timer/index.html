BJJ Timer – Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

BJJ Timer is a round timer for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu training. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect

None. BJJ Timer does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your timer settings (round length, rest time, cues and similar preferences) are saved only on your device so they are there next time you open the app. Deleting the app removes them.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android) and Live Activities (iPhone): used only to show the round countdown on the lock screen while a session runs.
  • Background audio: lets the bells and cues play while your phone is locked during a session.
  • Vibration: haptic cues at round changes.

The app does not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
